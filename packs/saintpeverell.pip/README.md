# Pip, the jumper

An original enemy for Captivity Reloaded Mod API v1, by Saint Peverell, in development. Pip is a small animatronic teddy in a top hat who scurries at you on frantic little steps, like a wind-up toy with glitch stutters, crouches as a tell and leaps to cling on. He never backs off. The pack uses only official Mod API v1 fields; validate the folder with **Captivity Reloaded > Modding > Captivity Mod Packager > Validate Mod**.

- **Rig:** his own, with Core bone names so Core motion retargets onto it, 2.1 units from sole to head top (his template's 1.85, drawn 1.15 times larger, up to the player's chest) plus his hat. Chest and belly are two pieces on a `spine` bone; the top hat (`hat`), bow tie (`bow-tie`) and ears are bones of their own, so they can wobble and get knocked crooked; the eyelid is a sprite swap (`eyelid-open`, `eyelid-half`, `eyelid-shut`) for glitch blinks. Hips is the single top-level bone. His genitals hang from the hips: `balls` and a shaft in three hinged pieces (`penis`, `penis-mid`, `penis-tip`). They are soft (`sheath` on `balls`, the shaft `empty`) until he catches the player and out (`cockbone-base`, `cockbone-mid`, `cockbone-tip`, `balls`) through the finisher. Every clip keys them at its start (`PipExampleGenerator`). In the finisher the shaft goes between the player's thighs through the hold and phase 2, springs out in phase 3's pull-back, is aimed by his hand in phase 4 and goes inside her at phase 5's slam until he pulls out in phase 7. Its pieces are drawn behind her near thigh and body while inside her outline (and all of it while it is inside her), and over his body while clear of her, always under his near leg so it is between his legs; the balls are on the shaft's layer, one under its base. `finisher/genital-motion.py` keys these per clip. The shaft is grey silicone. The atlas also holds the artist's other designs for clips to swap to: `cockbone-2-*`, `dogbone` (shown with `balls` empty) and `bonebone`.
- **Art:** a stand-in atlas made with an AI image tool (`ModSDK/EnemyTemplates/Pip/ai-placeholder`), except the genitals, which are the artist's final sprites (`artist/genitals`, cut into regions by `artist/cut-genitals.py`; `finalRegions` in `authoring/art-status.json` lists them). The owner approved shipping this atlas as it is (`releasable: true` in the art status, 2026-11-10), so `Tools/Release/Test-ShippableArt.ps1 -Pack ExampleMods/pip` passes; final sprites will replace it region for region. The parts sheet for the artist (names, sizes at 32 PPU, pivots and a reference image) is in `ModSDK/EnemyTemplates/Pip/artist/`.
- **Animation:**
  - His idle is the animation pipeline's. The run (move) and the leap are Sol's clips. The leap is a catch that, when it misses, hands over to a faceplant and push-up.
  - A catch starts his finisher, which has eight phase slots. Pip clings to the player's torso through the slots and lets go in the last one.
  - Slots 2 to 7 now use paired, authored clips from the approved three-beat workshop reference: irregular loop, held anticipation, one-arm locator adjustment, single drive, faster loop, and final burst with settling. Slots 1 and 8 remain the existing toolkit hold/release.
  - The finisher's sounds are Core's (soft slaps between her thighs, wet thrusts once he is in her, the player's voice and climax), on the workshop's beats (`finisher/finisher-sounds.py`), copied into `assets/audio`.
  - It reacts like a Core grab (experimental `reactions` and `startFreezeSeconds`). The catch freezes the game for half a second. Each stroke is a thrust cue: it shakes the camera, flushes the screen, opens her mouth and raises her pleasure and libido, and it plays a sound from the phase's pool with her voice at a chance that climbs through the act. His climax and pulses are cum thrusts, and phase 7 is the point of no return.
  - The middle slots no longer use placeholder holds. See `ModSDK/EnemyTemplates/Pip/finisher/README.md` and `finisher/workshop/README.md` for clips, authoring and validation.
- **Stage:** he comes from the switch on his own `Sandbox: Pip` stage (`saintpeverell.pip:stage/sandbox-pip`, in this pack, so it only appears when he is installed). He also comes to Furry Entertainment Robotics from wave 5: he joins its Abby spawners at half weight (`spawn.joinCoreSpawners` on `core:enemy/abby`, game 2.2.3 or later). Abby's spawners only switch on once the player opens the basement door, and wave 5 is always FER's Animatronic Spotlight wave, so he first comes in wave 6. The 2.2.3 release itself ignores minimum waves on Core stages (fixed by Captivity-Reloaded PR #36), so there he can come from wave 1.
